Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What industries do CARIYA Global's career pathways cover?

Career Pathways covers hospitality, tourism, aviation and travel — the four core industries in CARIYA Global's ecosystem.

How is Career Pathways different from Hospitality Careers?

Career Pathways compares routes across all four industries, while Hospitality Careers focuses specifically on hospitality roles.

Does CARIYA Global guarantee a career outcome?

No. CARIYA Global does not guarantee employment, placement, salary or career outcomes in any industry; these depend on individual performance, employers and market conditions.

How do I decide which career pathway is right for me?

Students can compare industry pages (hospitality, tourism, aviation, travel), review related courses, and contact CARIYA Global for guidance suited to their interests. for specific assessment tools.

Do career pathways include international opportunities?

CARIYA Global's ecosystem spans India (Chandigarh) as well as core Asian destinations including Singapore, Thailand and Malaysia. for specific international placement details.

What role do internships play in a career pathway?

Internship pathways run across hospitality, tourism and aviation, usually attached to a course pathway rather than standing alone. Availability depends on the destination, season and each host organisation's own intake. CARIYA Global does not guarantee placement.

Can I switch career pathways after starting a course?

Specifics for your chosen pathway are confirmed with CARIYA Global during counselling.

Where can I get personalized career pathway guidance?

Students can contact CARIYA Global directly through the Contact page to discuss which pathway may suit their goals.
