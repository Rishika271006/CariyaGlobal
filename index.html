Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CARIYA Global?
CARIYA Global is an education-and-career ecosystem that guides students toward hospitality, tourism, aviation and travel pathways — covering courses, training, internships and careers — with a focus on building futures across Asia.
Is CARIYA Global a university or college?
No. CARIYA Global is not a university, college or degree-granting institution. It provides counselling, course guidance and application support connecting students to education and career pathways.
Which industries does CARIYA Global focus on?
CARIYA Global focuses on four core industries: Hospitality, Tourism, Aviation and Travel.
Which countries does CARIYA Global cover for study and careers?
CARIYA Global's core Asian destinations are Singapore, Thailand and Malaysia, alongside broader guidance across Asia (including Japan and South Korea), supported by a local hub in Chandigarh, India.
Does CARIYA Global guarantee jobs, visas or admissions?
No. CARIYA Global does not guarantee employment, visas, immigration outcomes or admissions. It provides counselling, guidance and application support only.
What does 'Explore. Experience. Belong.' mean?
It is CARIYA Global's brand promise, reflecting its approach of helping students explore industries and destinations, gain real experience through training and internships, and find a sense of belonging in their chosen career path across Asia.
How do I get started with CARIYA Global?
You can start by visiting the Start Your Journey page, or contacting CARIYA Global directly via email at info@cariyaglobal.com or WhatsApp/call at +91 91155 11250.
Is there a local CARIYA Global office I can visit?
Yes, CARIYA Global has a local guidance hub in Chandigarh for students and parents in the Chandigarh/Punjab region — located at SCO 64-65, Top Floor, Sector 34-A, Chandigarh.
