Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

CARIYA Global maintains clear, transparent procedures regarding course admissions, registration fees, and training commitments.

1. General Principles

Before committing any fees, candidates are provided with complete course information, schedule options, and fee structures. All payments must be made through documented institutional banking channels.

2. Registration & Administrative Fees

Initial seat reservation and application administrative charges cover direct processing, documentation review, and profile assessment. Unless stated otherwise in a specific programme agreement, administrative processing fees are non-refundable once documentation evaluation has commenced.

3. Course Cancellation Prior to Batch Commencement

If a candidate provides written notice of cancellation prior to the official batch start date, refund eligibility will be determined based on the programme tier and institutional agreement. Requests must be submitted via email to info@cariyaglobal.com.

4. Third-Party Institutional & Visa Fees

Any payments remitted directly to foreign universities, partner training academies, hotel partners, embassy visa consular divisions, or flight booking agencies are subject strictly to the cancellation policies of those independent entities. CARIYA Global cannot refund fees disbursed to external government or educational bodies.

5. Processing Timelines

Approved refunds are credited back to the original source bank account within 14 to 21 business days following formal administrative verification.
