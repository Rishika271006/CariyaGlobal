Aviation ManagementChandigarhAsiaAirline IndustryCareer PathwaysInternational Internships

Aviation is an operations industry before it is anything else. Behind every departure sits a chain of people handling check-in, baggage, boarding, ramp coordination, cargo, safety checks and customer service — and that chain runs every day, in every airport, regardless of weather or disruption. Aviation courses at CARIYA Global focus on that operational layer: the ground and service roles that airports and airlines hire into in volume.

These are aviation management and ground-services pathways. They are not pilot training, not cabin-crew licensing and not aircraft maintenance certification — those are regulated qualifications issued by aviation authorities and approved training organisations, not by CARIYA Global.

What you study

What Aviation Courses Cover

The ground a aviation programme covers, module by module.

Airport & Ground Operations

  • How a terminal runs from landside to airside
  • Check-in, boarding and gate coordination
  • Baggage handling and reconciliation basics
  • Turnaround timing and why delays cascade

Passenger Services

  • Check-in systems and passenger documentation checks
  • Handling disruption, delays and rebooking calmly
  • Assisting passengers with reduced mobility
  • Service standards under time pressure

Airline Commercial Basics

  • Fares, ticketing concepts and booking classes
  • Reservations workflow and common changes
  • Excess baggage and ancillary services
  • Working within airline policy and escalation

Safety, Security & Compliance

  • Why airside discipline is non-negotiable
  • Restricted items and screening awareness
  • Emergency procedure familiarity
  • Documentation accuracy and audit culture

Cargo & Logistics Awareness

  • Air cargo flow and documentation
  • Dangerous-goods awareness at concept level
  • Warehouse-to-aircraft handover
  • Freight forwarding touchpoints

Professional Communication

  • Aviation-standard English for service roles
  • Clear announcements and radio discipline concepts
  • Handling complaints without escalation
  • Grooming, presentation and interview readiness

Honest fit check

Who These Courses Suit

  • School leavers who want an operational, non-desk industry
  • People who work well under time pressure and fixed procedures
  • Anyone comfortable with shift work, including early mornings and nights
  • Candidates with clear spoken English who enjoy public-facing work
  • Career changers moving in from hospitality, retail or customer service

It is procedure-driven

Airports run on standard procedure. That is learnable — which is why structured training matters more here than natural talent.

It is shift-based, not seasonal

Airports operate every day of the year. The work pattern is unusual hours rather than a short season.

It rewards calm

The candidates who progress are the ones who stay level when a flight goes wrong. That is a trainable skill, and courses drill it.
