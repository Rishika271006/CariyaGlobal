What Is Hospitality Management?

Hospitality Management develops career-focused knowledge for the modern hospitality sector, combining core professional skills, operational understanding, digital workflows and awareness of emerging AI-powered applications.

What Will You Learn?

Hospitality Management Course modules build core knowledge for the hospitality sector, from day-to-day operations through to career-ready professional skills.

01. Hospitality Operations

  • Hospitality industry structure
  • Department coordination
  • Service operations
  • Operational workflows
  • Quality and service standards

02. Guest Experience & Customer Service

  • Guest communication
  • Customer-service principles
  • Complaint handling
  • Professional etiquette
  • Personalised guest experience

03. Front Office & Accommodation

  • Front office fundamentals
  • Reservations awareness
  • Check-in and check-out concepts
  • Accommodation services
  • Guest relations

04. Food & Beverage & Events

  • Food-service fundamentals
  • Restaurant operations
  • Banquet and event service
  • Guest experience
  • Service standards

05. Hospitality Sales, Marketing & Digital

  • Hospitality marketing fundamentals
  • Digital communication
  • Customer engagement
  • Sales awareness
  • Brand experience

06. Professional Skills & Career Readiness

  • Communication
  • Teamwork
  • Presentation
  • Problem solving
  • Workplace behaviour
  • Career readiness

AI-Powered Learning

Technology is changing the hospitality industry. CARIYA Global brings an AI-aware, AI-driven and AI-generative perspective to professional learning.

  • AI-powered guest communication
  • AI-generative hospitality marketing content
  • AI-assisted customer-service workflows
  • AI-enhanced guest experience ideas
  • Digital hospitality research and content
  • Data-assisted operational decision support

The objective is to combine industry knowledge, professional skills and emerging technology awareness.
