The aviation sector

What Is the Aviation Industry?

Aviation is an operations industry before it is anything else. Behind every departure sits a chain of people handling check-in, baggage, boarding, ramp coordination, cargo, safety checks and customer service — running every day, in every airport, regardless of disruption. CARIYA Global's aviation focus sits on that operational and service layer: the ground, passenger and administrative roles that airports and airlines hire into in volume.

Airport OperationsAirline OperationsPassenger ServicesGround HandlingAirport Customer ServiceCheck-in & BoardingAviation AdministrationAirline Sales & ServicesAirport HospitalityAviation Safety AwarenessAviation TechnologyCargo & Logistics

CARIYA Global's aviation pathways are aviation management and ground/passenger-services training. They are not pilot training, cabin-crew licensing or aircraft maintenance certification — those are regulated qualifications issued by aviation authorities and approved training organisations.
