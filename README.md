# CARIYA Global — Hospitality, Tourism, Aviation & Travel Careers Across Asia

Official website and packages control center for **CARIYA Global** ([cariyaglobal.com](https://www.cariyaglobal.com/)), connecting students and early-career professionals from Chandigarh & across India to career pathways, professional training, international internships, and study abroad destinations in Asia (Singapore, Thailand, Malaysia, Japan, South Korea).

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights

- **80+ Responsive Web Pages**: Full sitemap coverage including all 4 industry verticals, comprehensive course hubs, Asian destination guides, 24 regional city hubs across Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, J&K, and Uttarakhand, plus legal policies (Privacy Policy, Disclaimer, Terms & Conditions, Refund Policy).
- **Executive Design System**: Modern, clean corporate identity with Navy (`#0B2545`), Crimson Red (`#D62839`), and Crisp Whites, optimized for high readability and fast mobile rendering.
- **Dedicated Admin Portal (`admin.html`)**: Complete package management control center to **Add, Edit, and Delete** career, training, and internship packages with real-time browser storage (`localStorage`), JSON export/import backups, search filters, and status toggles.
- **Dynamic Public Packages Showcase (`packages.html`)**: Public-facing course and internship package catalog dynamically synchronized with the admin store.
- **Zero Mojibake & Zero Broken Links**: Audited and verified with 100% valid cross-links and responsive assets.
