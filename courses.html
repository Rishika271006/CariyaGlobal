Course FAQs

What courses does CARIYA Global offer?
Pathways span hospitality and hotel management, tourism management, aviation management and travel management, with 2, 3, 6 and 12-month options delivered online, offline or hybrid. The institutions and exact programme titles for a given destination are confirmed during counselling.
Where are CARIYA Global courses based?
CARIYA Global is hubbed in Chandigarh, India, with programme pathways connected to Asian destinations including Singapore, Thailand and Malaysia.
Does CARIYA Global offer international internships alongside courses?
Yes, CARIYA Global connects course pathways to international internship opportunities in hospitality, tourism and aviation; see the respective internship pages for details.
What is the cost of CARIYA Global courses?
Fees depend on course duration, programme structure, learning format and the pathway selected. CARIYA Global does not use one fixed fee for every programme — contact our advisors for current pricing on the option you're considering.
