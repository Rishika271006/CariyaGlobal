Education in Thailand

Thailand's relevance to hospitality and tourism education is straightforward: it operates one of the largest visitor economies in the world, across a wide range of formats — city hotels in Bangkok, beach and island resorts in the south, cultural and mountain tourism in the north. That range means a student can encounter several quite different hospitality operating models within one country.

The scale of the sector also means service roles are abundant and the industry is deeply embedded in the economy rather than being a niche employer.

Courses in Thailand

Programme types typically cover hotel and resort operations, food and beverage service, tourism and destination management, tour operations and guiding fundamentals, and spa and wellness hospitality, which is a notable specialism in the Thai market. Structures and durations vary by institution.

The institutions and exact programme titles available in Thailand at any given intake are confirmed during counselling, so you get the current list rather than one that may have moved on by the time you read it.

Internships in Thailand

Resort and hotel internships are common in Thailand and are often genuinely operational — you are more likely to be on a floor, in a restaurant or at a desk than observing from the side. For learning the actual mechanics of hospitality service, that is an advantage.

Two honest caveats. First, seasonality is real: the southern resort economy has pronounced high and low seasons, and internship availability follows demand. Second, any right to work or train under a given visa category is governed by Thai immigration and labour regulation, which must be verified officially for your specific situation rather than assumed.

Career Opportunities in Thailand

The employing sectors are hotels and resorts, food and beverage operations, tour operations and destination management companies, spa and wellness operations, and the MICE and events sector concentrated in Bangkok.

Realistically: Thai language ability substantially widens what is available. English is widely used in international hotel and tourism operations, but roles requiring customer-facing Thai will be closed without it. Employment for foreign nationals is subject to Thai work permit rules, which are set by government and are not something any provider can guarantee an outcome on.

CARIYA Global does not guarantee employment, placement, salary levels, work passes or visa outcomes. Immigration and employment rules are set by government authorities and change over time; verify them against official sources for your own situation.

What Studying in Thailand Is Actually Like

Cost of living is markedly lower than Singapore, which changes what a given budget makes possible. Food is inexpensive and excellent; transport in Bangkok is workable, though the city is large and traffic is heavy.

The bigger adjustment is language. Outside tourism zones and international workplaces, day-to-day life runs in Thai, and students who make an early effort with basic Thai report a substantially easier time. The service culture places high value on composure and politeness, which is itself part of the professional training.

Student testimonials will be published here once CARIYA Global has students who have completed a pathway and consented to share their experience. Nothing invented is presented as a real student's words.

Eligibility

Entry requirements are set by each institution. Hospitality and tourism pathways generally consider academic qualification level and English ability; some programmes include an interview or practical assessment. Specific requirements are confirmed with the institution concerned.

Timelines depend on the institution's own admission cycle and the processing times of the relevant visa authority. Neither is set by CARIYA Global.

What It Costs to Study in Thailand

Thailand is generally the most affordable of the three destinations on living costs, with tuition varying widely by institution and programme type. Accommodation, food and local transport are all significantly cheaper than Singapore. Budget still needs to account for the full duration including low-season months, visa-related costs and travel home.

Budget against the full cost of the stay, not tuition alone — that is the mistake that most often derails a plan mid-course.

What can I study in Thailand through CARIYA Global?

Does CARIYA Global guarantee a student visa for Thailand?

No. Student visas and immigration approvals for Thailand are decided solely by the Thai government and its immigration authorities; CARIYA Global does not control, influence or guarantee visa outcomes.

What are the eligibility requirements to study in Thailand with CARIYA Global?

Eligibility is 10th Pass or 12th Pass depending on the programme, and may vary according to the selected pathway and destination. Your specific eligibility is confirmed during counselling.

How much does it cost to study in Thailand?

Fees depend on course duration, programme structure, learning format and the pathway selected. CARIYA Global does not use one fixed fee for every programme — contact the team for current pricing on the option you're considering.

Can I get an internship in Thailand's hospitality or tourism industry through CARIYA Global?

Internship pathways run across hospitality, tourism and aviation, usually attached to a course pathway rather than standing alone. Availability depends on the destination, season and each host organisation's own intake. CARIYA Global does not guarantee placement.

Why do students choose Thailand for hospitality and tourism careers?

Thailand has a large, well-established tourism and hospitality sector, making it a popular destination for students seeking international exposure in these industries.

How do I apply to study in Thailand through CARIYA Global?

Start with a counselling conversation — no documents needed. From there: choose the pathway, prepare the documents the institution requires, apply with CARIYA Global's support, and if an offer is made, move on to visa and pre-departure steps. Timelines follow the institution's admission cycle and the visa authority's processing.
