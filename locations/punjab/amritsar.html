About Amritsar

One of India's genuine tourism cities, with an international airport.

Amritsar is among the most-visited cities in India. The Golden Temple draws enormous domestic and international footfall year-round, the Wagah border ceremony is a fixed visitor draw, and Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport connects the city directly to destinations across Asia, the Gulf and the UK.\n\nThat combination — high-volume religious tourism, an international airport, and a dense hotel and guesthouse economy — makes Amritsar unusually relevant for students in all four industries at once. Hospitality, tourism, aviation and travel are all visibly at work in the same city.

Students from Amritsar often already understand visitor-facing work from the city itself. That is a genuine head start when moving into international hospitality.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which courses are available for students in Amritsar?

Students from Amritsar can explore Hospitality, Tourism, Aviation and Travel pathways — including Hotel Management, Hospitality Management, Tourism Management, Aviation Management and Travel Management — in 2, 3, 6 and 12-month options.

Can I study hotel management in Amritsar?

Yes. Hotel Management and Hospitality Management pathways are open to students from Amritsar, delivered online, offline or hybrid depending on the programme. Which options apply to a given intake is confirmed during counselling.

Can students from Amritsar apply for international internships?

Yes. Internship pathways to Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, South Korea and Japan are open to students from Amritsar, subject to programme eligibility, host organisation selection and the destination's immigration rules. Placement is never guaranteed.

Does CARIYA Global have an office in Amritsar?

CARIYA Global's office is in Chandigarh at SCO 64-65, Top Floor, Sector 34-A. Students from Amritsar are supported by phone and WhatsApp, and in person at the Chandigarh hub.

Which course is best after Class 12 in Amritsar?

There is no single best course. The right choice depends on whether your interest is hotels and guest service, tourism and destinations, airports and airlines, or travel and ticketing — which is exactly what a counselling conversation is for.

Does CARIYA Global guarantee a job for Amritsar students?

No. CARIYA Global provides counselling, course guidance and application support. It does not guarantee admission, internship placement, employment, salary or visa approval.
