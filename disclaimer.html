Disclaimer

Last Updated: September 2026

The information provided by CARIYA Global on this website is intended for general educational, career-development and informational purposes.

While we make reasonable efforts to keep information useful and accurate, programme details can change and should be independently verified before making a decision.

1. Programme Information

Course and programme information may include course duration, fees, eligibility, curriculum, learning mode, certification, intake dates, internship opportunities and career pathways. These details may vary according to institution, programme, destination, batch and seat availability.

2. No Admission Guarantee

Information about a course or institution does not constitute a guarantee of admission. Final admission decisions depend exclusively on the relevant institution's eligibility requirements, academic assessment, documentation verification, interview evaluations, seat availability and internal policies.

3. No Placement or Employment Guarantee

CARIYA Global does not guarantee job placement, internship placement, employment, employer selection, salary brackets, promotion or guaranteed career advancement. Career outcomes depend entirely on individual student diligence, academic merit, communication skills, language proficiency, employer requirements, and external economic conditions.

4. Salary Information

Where salary ranges or career earnings are presented, they should be treated as indicative information based on general industry observations rather than guaranteed compensation. Actual compensation varies broadly based on role, employer, experience, destination country, skills, and candidate performance.

5. Internship Disclaimer

Internship opportunities vary according to host organisation, destination, availability, candidate profile, programme requirements and employer selection. An internship should not be interpreted as a guaranteed paid position or automatic future employment offer.

6. Visa & Immigration Disclaimer

CARIYA Global does not guarantee student visa approval, internship visa approval, work permit approval, immigration clearance, or entry into any foreign country. All visa and immigration decisions are rendered exclusively by the competent government and diplomatic authorities of the host nation. Applicants must review and comply with official consulate regulations.

7. Accreditation Disclaimer

CARIYA Global will identify accreditation, certification, or institutional affiliation only where such information has been officially confirmed and documented. A course appearing on the CARIYA Global website should not automatically be interpreted as government-accredited or university-affiliated unless expressly documented.

8. Third-Party Information

The website may reference universities, vocational academies, training organisations, hotel brands, airlines, employers, and government departments. CARIYA Global is not responsible for policy changes made by independent third parties.

9. AI-Generated & Digital Information

CARIYA Global may use digital and artificial intelligence technologies to assist in educational pathway mapping and content presentation. Such information is provided for guidance and should not substitute for official regulatory or professional advice.

10. Information Changes

Course schedules, tuition fees, entry rules, and visa procedures are subject to dynamic updates. Candidates are advised to confirm all specifics with our team before committing funds or booking travel.

11. CARIYA Global's Commitment

Our foundation is: Clear Information. Verified Facts. Real Career Pathways.
Where facts have not been verified, we clearly designate them rather than presenting assumptions as established certainty.
