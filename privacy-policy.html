Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

CARIYA Global ("CARIYA Global", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how CARIYA Global may collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, apply for a course or internship, request counselling, or interact with our services.

By using the CARIYA Global website or submitting information through our forms, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with CARIYA Global, we may collect information such as:

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number & WhatsApp number
  • City and country of residence
  • Date of birth, where required
  • Academic qualifications and education background
  • Career interests and industry preferences
  • Course, internship, and destination preferences

Application Information

Where relevant to a programme or service, we may receive information relating to:

  • Course and internship application forms
  • Educational certificates and marksheets
  • CV / Resume and professional portfolio
  • Work experience history
  • Passport or travel-related information where required for institutional admissions or visa verification

You should only provide documents and information that are specifically requested or reasonably required for the relevant service.

2. Information You Provide Through Forms

When you use forms such as Contact Us, Enquire Now, Career Counselling, Course Enquiry, Internship Enquiry, Application Forms, or Newsletter and Updates, we may collect the information you voluntarily submit.

This information is used strictly to understand your requirements and respond promptly with accurate guidance.

3. How We Use Your Information

CARIYA Global may use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries regarding hospitality, tourism, aviation and travel programs
  • Provide personalized course guidance and educational recommendations
  • Discuss international internship opportunities across Asian destinations
  • Process admissions and enrollment applications
  • Communicate timetable, batch, and intake updates
  • Provide destination, travel, and campus information
  • Connect you with verified institutional or training representatives
  • Improve website functionality, accessibility, and user experience
  • Maintain internal administrative records
  • Fulfill statutory, administrative, or legal requirements

We do not sell personal information to third parties, nor do we use personal details for unrelated purposes without appropriate consent.

4. Communication

If you submit an enquiry, you may receive communication through channels such as email, telephone, WhatsApp, SMS, or direct messaging provided by you. Communication will relate to your enquiry, course application, or requested counseling. You may at any time request that we pause or stop non-essential promotional messages.

5. Sharing of Information

CARIYA Global may share relevant information with authorised third parties where strictly necessary to provide a requested service. For example:

  • Educational institutions and partner academies
  • Training providers and vocational bodies
  • Internship hosts and hotel / airline employer partners
  • Technology, CRM, and communication service providers
  • Payment gateways and banking partners
  • Professional advisors and regulatory authorities when legally obligated

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may utilize verified third-party tools such as analytics providers, secure cloud hosting, communication suites, and payment processors. These entities operate according to their respective privacy standards. CARIYA Global does not govern the independent practices of third-party platforms.

7. Cookies & Analytics

CARIYA Global may utilize cookies and similar digital technologies to assess traffic patterns, store preferences, enhance website performance, and maintain security. Users may adjust their cookie permissions via their browser settings.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard organizational and technological safeguards to shield your data against unauthorized access, theft, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission can be guaranteed as 100% immune from security threats.

9. Data Retention

Personal details are stored for as long as required to administer requested courses, handle ongoing applications, satisfy accounting or legal rules, and resolve disputes.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable data protection legislation, you are entitled to request access to your records, rectify errors, request deletion, or withdraw specific consent. To exercise your rights, please contact our team directly.

11. Children's Information

Where programs involve students who have completed 10th or 12th standard and are below the legal age of majority, information should be provided with the active involvement and consent of a parent or guardian.

12. Changes & Contact Details

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of our site reflects acceptance of updated guidelines.
